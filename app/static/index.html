MelodyMate

Music learning, made accessible.

Welcome to MelodyMate

Explore music through singing, piano keys, and guided listening exercises. You can use a keyboard or supported voice commands.

The spoken guide starts when this page opens. Choose a module by speaking or pressing a number. Press R to repeat a question and Escape to stop. No pointer is needed. Voice recognition requires an internet connection and works best in supported browsers.

Settings

MelodyMate uses a gentle voice when one is available in your browser. Practice reports are read slowly to make them easier to follow.

If you use a screen reader, consider leaving this option off to avoid overlapping speech.

Singing practice

Listen to the reference melody, then sing it into your microphone. Headphones help prevent the reference audio from being picked up during recording.

Practice and recording become available after you confirm that you are wearing headphones.

Keyboard learning — all 88 keys

Explore a standard piano from A0 to C8. Select an octave to focus on a smaller section, or explore the entire keyboard.
